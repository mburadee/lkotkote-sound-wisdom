# Architecture Rules

- Keep the Sound Map species-first and powered by Leaflet with keyless OpenStreetMap tiles, because habitat and audio should remain the primary experience without provider credentials.
- Resolve exact accepted species names through GBIF and display its global occurrence tiles with attribution, because recorded presence must never be presented as an invented or complete habitat boundary.
- Keep original bird photo asset pointers separate from source-credit metadata and cutouts, because untouched photographs must retain their photographer attribution without reusing edited images.
